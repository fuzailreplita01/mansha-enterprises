## 1. Logo

The logo is an abstract geometric **M** mark paired with the MANSHA wordmark. The mark is built on a 1 : 1.618 golden-ratio grid.

**Files in this kit**

| File | Use |
|------|-----|
| `logo-color.png` | Full-color stacked logo (transparent) — for light backgrounds |
| `logo-white.png` | White / mono stacked logo (transparent) — for dark backgrounds & photos |
| `logo-horizontal.png` | Wide horizontal lockup — for site headers on light backgrounds |
| `logo-horizontal-white.png` | Wide horizontal lockup — for dark headers |
| `favicon.png` | 512×512 square app icon / favicon |

**When to use which version**
- Use the **color** version on white or light (`#F4F3EE`) backgrounds.
- Use the **white** version on dark backgrounds (`#0A0A0A`), photos, or busy imagery.
- Use the **horizontal** lockup in site headers and navigation bars.
- Use the **favicon** tile for browser tabs, app icons, and social avatars.

**Minimum size**
- Stacked logo: no smaller than **120px** wide on screen.
- Horizontal logo: no smaller than **160px** wide.
- Favicon: scales down to **32px**; keep the standalone mark (no wordmark) below that.

**Clear space**
Keep empty space equal to the **height of the M mark** on all sides. Nothing (text, images, edges) should enter this zone.

## 5. Spacing

Base unit: **8px**. Use multiples for all margins, padding, and gaps.

`4 · 8 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96`

- Component internal padding: 16–24px
- Gaps between elements: 16–24px
- Section vertical padding: 64–96px

---

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

**Do**
- Keep generous clear space around the logo.
- Use electric yellow as an accent — let black and off-white carry most of the layout.
- Pair Space Grotesk headings with IBM Plex Sans body for clear hierarchy.

**Don't**
- Don't place the yellow mark on a light/white background without the dark wordmark — contrast is too low alone.
- Don't recolor, stretch, rotate, or add effects (shadows, gradients, outlines) to the logo.
- Don't fill a layout with large blocks of electric yellow — it's an accent, not a background.
